# 01 — Hearing Loss Disparities

**Working title**: Racial and Geographic Disparities in Documented Hearing Loss and Related Care at UMMC / Across Mississippi Counties  
**Status**: Ideation → early draft  
**Primary data**: UMMC Patient Cohort Explorer / RDW (confirmed access) + CDC PLACES (hearing disability) + County Health Rankings for context  
**Target**: Local/regional conference abstract (MAFP, ACP MS chapter, UMMC Research Day) → possible full paper later

## Specific aims (draft — refine after first RDW queries)

1. Describe the prevalence and demographic distribution (race, age, sex, residence) of patients with documented hearing loss diagnoses in the UMMC RDW.
2. Examine disparities in utilization of audiology/ENT services among those patients, stratified by race and (if available) rural vs non-rural residence.
3. Place UMMC findings in the context of county-level hearing disability estimates (CDC PLACES) and primary-care access metrics across Mississippi.

## Why this topic
- Clear ENT niche that is still under-studied at the county / health-system level in Mississippi.
- Strong public-data complement (PLACES hearing disability measure).
- Feasible with existing RDW access + free public files.
- Locally relevant given rural access barriers and known disparities in sensory impairment.
