# Conference abstract targets (late 2026–2027)

**Purpose:** First-author trainee (UMMC medical student) venues for Paper 01 (hearing-loss / audiology access disparities; UMMC RDW) and Papers 02–04 (county-level public-data work on oral/pharynx plus breast/colorectal cancer, PCP supply and premature mortality, and birth outcomes / obstetric access).  
**Rules used:** Official pages only; if a 2027 deadline was not posted, the most recent year’s deadline is labeled as such. Project abstract standard is ≤500 words including section labels. Several venues are tighter (see notes).

## Notes

- **UMMC Research Day:** Do not use February 23 or March 31 as 2027 dates. The FAQ still says 2020; the eligibility page dropped the year but kept the same weekday/date pair that matches 2020, not 2026 or 2027.
- **SGIM Southern event page typo:** The official call lists the meeting as February 18–20, 2027. The event page title also says 2027 and New Orleans, but its “Event Date” field incorrectly shows February 18–20, 2026. The 2027 dates from the call-for-submissions page are used here.
- **AcademyHealth:** Prefer the official ARM 2027 page date of January 12, 2027, and confirm against the live Confex portal (which mentions January 14 in its confirmation-step text).
- **Prior publication / prior presentation:** Several venues (SGIM, Triological, AMA, MAFP, SNMA, AAFP FUTURE) have rules about prior publication or prior presentation. Check each policy before dual-submitting Paper 01.
- **Membership often required:** MAFP/AAFP, ACP (national and often chapter), AMA Research Challenge, SNMA (WCJRF), and APHA (if accepted). SMA PIT and AAS mentored posters are trainee-oriented; AAS new-investigator awards require AAS membership.
- **MSMA:** The annual session is a House of Delegates meeting, not a research poster conference, on the evidence reviewed.
- **Delta Region:** There is no 2027 open-call “Delta Region rural health” abstract meeting posted. The closest working options are NRHA 2027 in New Orleans (January 15, 2027), MSRHA’s in-state conference (watch for a 2027 call), and the Delta Regional Forum (invited-style program, no open call found).
- Re-check official pages before submitting; some sites update or extend deadlines without much notice.
